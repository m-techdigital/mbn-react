# Known limitations
Mini is a one-admin-many-customers marketplace storefront. It intentionally excludes RBAC graphs, company/department/project scope, HR, accounting, BI reports, CRM reservations and generic workflow automation.

Current bounded scope includes customer auth/profile, product listing, sell/rent transaction journeys, wallet deposit/withdrawal, payout tracking, media upload and customer-facing document/history views.

Approval, publication and availability are represented only through the Mini product/transaction lifecycle. Do not add a separate approval-center or inventory module unless a real MBN consumer is approved.
